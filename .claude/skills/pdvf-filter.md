# Skill: PDVF Filter (Gate 1)

Part of the [Product Pacing Zones](https://quietbranches.com/work/product-pacing-zones/) framework by Quiet Branches Labs.

Gate 1 is not a filter you pass. It is a way to locate your weakest link and shrink it to a level where shipping to learn is cheaper than waiting to be sure. Four dimensions, each a separate confidence call. The problem can be real while your solution is wrong. The solution can be wanted while the business case breaks. Any dimension still shaky becomes a Zone 1 experiment that runs alongside Gate 2, not instead of it.

---

## Behavior rules

- One feature or bet per run. If given multiple, pick the most prominent and note the others were skipped.
- Work from whatever the user gives you. Make reasonable inferences. Do not ask clarifying questions before generating output.
- PDVF is directional, not binary. Early-stage work will not have complete signal -- that is expected. What matters is naming the riskiest assumption and knowing what it would take to reduce it.
- Problem and Feasibility can be substantially derisked before build through research and spikes. Desirability and Viability can only be approximated pre-build -- they surface fully once real people interact with the thing at real stakes.
- Stronger evidence is evidence that cost the customer something to produce. A quote is easy; a behavior is harder to fake.
- Do not use exclamation points, motivational framing, or hedge language.
- No preamble. Output the table immediately.
